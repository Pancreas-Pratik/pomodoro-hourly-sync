Privacy Policy

Pomodoro Hourly Synchronization · Last updated October 23, 2026

What the app handles

The timer works locally on your device. Appearance and sound preferences are saved in your browser's local storage so they can be remembered. The app has no developer-operated account system, analytics, advertising, or database.

Inside Discord, the app may temporarily read and display the names of people in the same Activity. It does not save those names or send them to a developer-operated server.

Third-party services

The app is hosted on GitHub Pages and may run inside Discord. GitHub and Discord may process technical information, such as IP addresses, according to their own privacy policies: GitHub and Discord.

Your choices and contact

Change preferences in the app or clear this site's browser storage to erase saved preferences. The developer has no app database containing user information to delete. For questions, use the project's GitHub Issues; do not post sensitive information publicly.
